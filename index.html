OUR STORY, OUR PURPOSE

More than ice cream.
A scoop of change.

School days can be busy. Choosing a quick snack is easy; finding a healthier option isn’t always as simple. We believe students deserve something that fits their day and brings them joy.

That’s why we created Miau’s Cream, a student entrepreneurship project with a simple idea: make healthier food choices delicious, practical, and appealing to young people.

Because building better habits can start with something you already love.

A SCHOOL COMMUNITY. A SHARED DREAM.

Small team.
Big-hearted idea.

We’re Thadeo, Camila, Annia, and Elma. This project helps us learn teamwork, responsibility, and creativity while building something with a positive purpose.

With nutritionist and marketing support, and encouragement from parents and our school community, we hope to inspire healthier habits—one scoop at a time.
